0020hREADEvery 100 ms (poll)Status Word: bit6=Running, bit3=Fault, bit0=Ready
0025hREADEvery 100 ms (poll)Output Frequency (×0.01 Hz, e.g. 0x0FA0 = 40.00 Hz)
0021hREADEvery 100 ms (poll)Output Current (×0.1 A)
